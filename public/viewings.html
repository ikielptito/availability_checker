How viewings work

Villa viewings, coordinated for you.

One WhatsApp message books a viewing. Maya checks the time with whoever runs the villa, both sides get a calendar invite, and a reminder lands on the morning of the visit. No chasing, no mix-ups.

For agents

Booking a viewing for your client

1
Ask Maya on WhatsApp
Tell her the villa and when your client wants to see it — "Can we view Villa Amara Thursday afternoon?" is enough.
2
Maya checks with the villa
She contacts whoever manages the villa and asks them to confirm your slot. You don't wait on hold — she comes back to you the moment they answer.
3
Confirmed — with a calendar invite
When the villa confirms, you and the villa contact both receive the confirmation and a one-tap calendar link, so the viewing sits in both calendars in your own timezone.
4
Reminder on the day
On the morning of the viewing, Maya reminds you and the villa contact. After the visit, tell her how it went — it keeps the villa's record sharp for your next deal.
For villa owners & managers

When an agent wants to view your villa

1
Maya messages you the request
You get one WhatsApp message: an agent would like to view your villa, with the day and time they asked for.
2
One tap to answer
The request comes with three buttons — Confirm ✓, Different time, or Can't this time. Tap one and it's done; or just type your own reply, that works too. Nothing is confirmed until you say so.
3
You both get the invite and a reminder
Once you confirm, you and the agent each get a calendar link, and Maya reminds you both on the morning of the visit. Then the agent shows your villa — you close the deal directly with them.
Good to know

The small print, in plain words

Where to see your viewings
Agents: open your profile on sambarentals.com — your viewings are listed with their status and calendar links. Owners: sign in at sambarentals.com/portal and open the Viewings tab. Both update live as things are confirmed.
The calendar invite
The confirmation message carries two links: a Google Calendar link (one tap on most phones) and an Apple/other-calendars file. Times are set in Bali time and your phone shows them in your own timezone automatically.
Need to reschedule or cancel?
Just tell Maya on WhatsApp. She updates the other side, and if the viewing was already in your calendar, you'll get an updated link.
Agents: your client stays yours
Maya never asks for or shares your client's details. The villa side only ever sees that "an agent" requested the viewing — the relationship, and the full 10% commission, stay with you.
No reply from the villa?
Maya nudges once, and if there's still no answer she tells you honestly instead of leaving you hanging — so you can offer your client a different villa in time.
