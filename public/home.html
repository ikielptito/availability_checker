Our philosophy

Things we deliberately don't do

We're not trying to be everything. These boundaries are what make Samba trustworthy.

We don't compete with agents. Samba connects owners with agents — we never go around them. Agents are the network.
We don't replace Airbnb. Samba covers the long-term stay market that Airbnb doesn't serve well. We sync with your existing calendar.
We don't take commissions. No booking fees, no referral cuts. Just IDR 150k/month per villa — you keep what you earn.
We don't middleman your deals. When an agent has an interested client, they reach you directly to arrange villa viewings and close the deal. Samba makes the introduction, then stays out of the way.
We don't become another marketplace. No guest reviews, no instant booking, no race to the bottom. Quality inventory, trusted network.
