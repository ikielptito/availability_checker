What changed in September 2026

We rebuilt how villas are prepared and checked. Every villa now has a written readiness standard, every guest arrival is preceded by a photo check that our assistant verifies, every fortnight the villa is inspected with photos that reach you on your weekly report, and every repair is a ticket you approve or decline from your phone. One rule behind all of it: nothing about your villa should be a surprise to you. If a repair needs money, you hear before it is done. If a guest complains, we have the photos from before they arrived. If a month's rent is in, the statement says so.

2 · Maya

Maya: what she sends you, and what you can ask her

Maya is Samba's assistant on WhatsApp. She is not a person; she is software run by our team, and she says so if you ask. She messages you only about your villa, and only when something needs you or is ready for you.

What she sends

  • Repair approvals. A repair with a cost, one tap to approve or decline. Nothing that costs money is done without your yes.
  • Repair notices. Routine work we are doing anyway, or a heads-up about something found today whose cost follows.
  • Repair completed. When the work is done, with the final cost.
  • Your monthly statement, when it is published, with a link.
  • Your weekly report, every Monday: views, enquiries, bookings, occupancy, and the inspection photos from that week.
  • Your portal link, when you need to sign in.

What you can ask her

Reply to any message, in English or Indonesian. She knows your statements, your bookings, your open repairs and the housekeeping schedule. Examples: "What was August's payout?", "Is anyone staying this week?", "What happened with the leak?", "When is the next inspection?". If she cannot answer, she tells you and passes it to Era or Ikiel.

Maya → you
Hi Cielo, a repair at Tropicana Valley Unit A5 needs your approval: Replace glassware — long glass, stemmed glasses and bowl Estimated cost: IDR 85,000 Tap to review and approve. It appears as an expense on the month's statement once done.
Review and approve
Approved. Can you also tell me when the leak by the back door is being looked at?
Thank you. The leak was found on Saturday's inspection; Era is getting a quote and you will get it here to approve. I will let you know the moment it is in.

What she will not do: spend your money, change your prices, or message your guests. Those stay with you, Era and Ikiel.

3 · Your owner portal

Your owner portal

sambarentals.com/portal. Sign in with your WhatsApp number: type it, tap the link Maya sends, and you are in. No password. You stay signed in for 30 days on that phone or computer.

My properties: each villa you own with Samba, its listing, and who is on its contact list.

Maintenance: every repair ticket, what it costs, and the Approve button for the ones waiting on you.

The five tabs

1

My properties. Your villas, their live listing, views and enquiries over the last week or month, and the contacts we send reports to.

2

Weekly reports. Every Monday's report, kept. Interest in the listing, bookings and revenue, occupancy ahead, and the inspection photos with any repairs they raised.

3

Statements. Each month's payout statement: rent received, our fee, expenses, what is owed to you, and whether it has been paid. Download as Excel for your accountant.

4

Maintenance. All repairs: waiting for your approval, approved and in progress, completed with the final cost. Approve or decline here or from Maya's message, whichever is closer.

5

Housekeeping. Every cleaning visit, every photo check before a guest and every inspection round at your villa, with the photos and a PDF of each record, plus the next planned clean, inspection and deep clean.

Co-owners can have their own sign-in on the same villa. Ask Ikiel to add their number.

4 · The cleaning and readiness standard

The cleaning and readiness standard

What every Samba villa gets, and how we know it was done.

1

Cleaned twice a week, occupied or notOn fixed weekdays per villa, whether a guest is in or the villa is empty. An empty villa still has to show well for viewings.

2

A turnover clean the day a guest leaves, and a preparation clean before the next one arrivesPrepared the day before arrival when the villa has stood empty for five days or more, so a guest never walks into a villa that has been closed up for a week.

3

A photo check before every guestWhen the housekeeper finishes preparing the villa, she photographs seven fixed spots: the living room with the sofa covers on, the kitchen and the inside of the oven, the bathroom with soap and shower gel stocked, the bedroom made, the wall that was dirtiest, the pool area and its furniture, and the consumables box. Our assistant checks the photos against the standard before the guest arrives. Anything not right is fixed while she is still there. A villa nobody photographed is treated as not ready.

4

Stocked when the guest arrivesEvery villa has a consumables box: hand soap, shower gel, shampoo, dish soap and sponge, kitchen roll, toilet roll, bin bags, and a dispenser gallon with two sealed bottles of water beside it. The housekeeper counts it at every turnover and Era restocks.

5

Spare sofa covers in every villaA cover being washed never leaves a sofa bare when a guest walks in.

6

Every handover is on recordThe photos, the check result and the housekeeper's name are kept permanently, dated, and tied to the booking before and after. That record is what protects you in a damage dispute.

Why this exists. A guest judges a villa in the first ten minutes: a bare sofa, an empty soap bottle, a rusty oven, a stained wall. One bad review costs months of bookings. The standard above is written from exactly those complaints.

5 · Inspections, deep cleans, minimum kit

Inspections, deep cleans and the minimum kit

Every two weeks: an inspection with photos

The housekeeper walks the villa and photographs the bathroom, the ceilings, the wall behind the air conditioner, the kitchen and the pool, looking for mould, damp and damage. She also tests what a photo cannot show: door locks and hinges, every plug and switch, the air conditioner and TV remote, curtains, taps and showers, the hob, fridge and dispenser, the safe, the fire extinguisher date, hangers. Anything wrong becomes a repair ticket. The photos and findings appear on your weekly report, and "we looked and it was fine" is reported too.

Every three months: a deep clean

All walls and skirting, inside the oven, exterior windows including upstairs, bathroom grout, pool cushions washed, the water dispenser sanitised, under and behind furniture. Scheduled automatically on a day the villa is empty when one is coming, and also the day after any guest who stayed three weeks or more. Stains that will not wipe off come to you as a repaint ticket.

The minimum kit

Guests compare your villa with the one next door. These ten items are the Samba standard for every villa we manage. Era audits each villa once; anything missing reaches you as a repair ticket with a cost, to approve like any other.

KettleMicrowave
Full-length mirrorIron and ironing board
Towel rack or ladderDrying rack by the pool
HairdryerSpare set of sofa covers
Water dispenser, hot and cold, with a dated sanitised stickerPool furniture cushions clean, no mould

Villas without an oven or a pool are photographed and checked for what they have. The standard adapts; the housekeeper is told what to do in each case.

6 · Repairs

Repairs: from a photo to an approved job

Every repair is a ticket. You see it, you decide on it, and you see it closed.

1

Found. A housekeeper's photo during a clean or inspection, a guest's message, or Era's own visit. The ticket is created with the photo and the villa.

2

Assessed. Era looks and adds an estimated cost. For something urgent, a leak for example, you get a heads-up from Maya the same day, before the cost is known.

3

Your decision. Maya sends the ticket with the cost and a button. Approve or Not right now, from the message or from the portal's Maintenance tab. Small routine work we are doing anyway comes as a notice, not a question.

4

Done. Era sends our repair team, and Maya chases until it is finished. You get a completion message with the final cost.

5

On the statement. The cost appears as an expense on the month the work was done, with the ticket's title, so the statement and the messages always match.

The three kinds of message
Approval: "…needs your approval. Estimated cost IDR 850,000. Tap to review."
Notice: "…we are doing this as routine work, just so you know."
Heads-up: "…found by our team today, being assessed; the repair cost follows for your approval."

Nothing is spent without you. Declining is fine and costs nothing; the ticket stays on record as declined. If you want a second quote or your own contractor, reply to Maya and say so.

Repair costs are passed through at cost. Our management fee is the 10% on rent and nothing else.

7 · Statements and the weekly report

Statements, payouts and the weekly report

The Monday report: interest in the listing, this week at a glance, bookings and revenue, occupancy ahead. Inspection photos and repairs appear in the weeks a round happened.

The monthly statement

Published once the month's rent is in. It lists every booking and the rent received, our 10% management fee, expenses with their tickets, any adjustment, and the amount owed to you. When we transfer it, the statement shows paid. Download it as Excel for your accountant from the Statements tab.

Rent is quoted to guests and agents with the agent's 10% already inside it, so the rent on your statement is what was actually received.

The weekly report

Every Monday, on WhatsApp and kept in the portal. It is built from live data: listing views and enquiries, agents who shared the villa, new bookings and nights sold, occupancy for the next 30 days, and the fortnightly inspection with its photos and any repairs raised.

A question about a number? Ask Maya in the same chat: "why is August lower than July?" She answers from the statement itself.

8 · Records and questions

Records, damage claims and questions

Every clean, check and inspection is kept

Open the Housekeeping tab in your portal. Each cleaning visit, each photo check before a guest and each fortnightly inspection is listed with its date and outcome. Tap View the photos to see what the housekeeper sent, and Download as PDF for a dated record with the photos and the bookings around it. Nothing in a record can be edited after the fact. The cards at the top show the next planned clean, inspection and deep clean.

Housekeeping: what is planned next, then the log. "Flagged, then fixed" means the photo check caught something and it was put right before the guest arrived.

If a guest damages something

Download the PDF from before that guest arrived and the one from after they left, and send both to the platform's claim or your insurer. Or tell Era or Maya and we prepare it for you.

If a guest complains

We answer the guest the same day, fix what is wrong as fast as it can be fixed, and tell you when it is done. If the complaint points at something structural, it becomes a ticket for you.

Questions we get most

Can I visit or stay in my villa?Yes. Tell Era the dates so the calendar is blocked and the villa is prepared for you like a guest.
Who pays for consumables?Soap, water and the like are part of running the villa and appear on the statement as expenses where they apply.
Can I use my own contractor?Yes. Decline the ticket and tell Maya; Era coordinates access.
Can my partner get the messages too?Yes. Ikiel adds their number; they get their own portal sign-in.
How do I change the price?Message Ikiel. Pricing is a conversation, not a form.
I do not want WhatsApp messages.Tell Ikiel. Reports and statements can go by email and the portal instead.

Anything else: reply to Maya. If it is hers to answer she does; if not, Era or Ikiel gets it with your message attached.
